## 🌱 Why SignalForge Exists

Morse code and the NATO phonetic alphabet are two of the most enduring communication systems ever devised by humanity. One relies on the purity of tone — dots and dashes transmitted across oceans. The other relies on the clarity of spoken word — Alfa, Bravo, Charlie — cutting through static and noise when every syllable matters.

Learning either system is deceptively simple on paper and surprisingly demanding in practice. SignalForge exists because memorizing a chart is not the same as *owning* a skill. The goal here is fluency: the ability to hear a sequence and know it instantly, or to speak a callsign without hesitation.

This project started as a personal challenge — a simple trainer to get quicker with Morse and the phonetic alphabet. It has since grown into a modular, extensible training environment suitable for solo practice, classroom drills, and hobbyist radio clubs.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

Think of SignalForge as a *gymnasium for your ears and voice*. It offers structured repetitions, timed challenges, adaptive difficulty, and progress telemetry — all wrapped in a calm, distraction-reduced interface.

Instead of overwhelming the learner with endless options, SignalForge focuses on three pillars:

1. **Recognition** — Hear it, see it, identify it.
2. **Reproduction** — Type it, tap it, speak it.
3. **Retention** — Spaced repetition keeps knowledge alive over months, not days.

Each pillar is reinforced by measurable feedback: reaction times, accuracy percentages, and streaks that reward consistency rather than raw talent.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

SignalForge ships with a rich feature set designed to accommodate beginners and seasoned operators alike.

- 🎯 **Adaptive Drill Engine** — Difficulty scales automatically based on your rolling accuracy window.
- 🔊 **Tone Synthesis & Playback** — Adjustable WPM (words per minute), Farnsworth spacing, and pure sine tone generation.
- ⌨️ **Multi-Input Support** — Practice via keyboard, on-screen paddle, or external key interface.
- 🗣️ **Phonetic Voice Drills** — Hear a callsign, respond with the correct NATO sequence.

- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** — Interface available in multiple languages with region-aware phonetic conventions.
- 📱 **Responsive UI** — Layouts adapt gracefully from desktops to tablets to pocket devices.
- 🔁 **Spaced Repetition Scheduler** — Items you struggle with resurface more often; mastered items fade into review.
- 🎧 **Custom Sound Profiles** — Save tone presets for different environments (quiet room, noisy field, classroom).
- 🛰️ **Offline-First Architecture** — No connection required for core drills; sync when convenient.
- 🧩 **Plugin-Ready Drill Modules** — Extend training with community-created exercises.

## 🎓 Training Modes

SignalForge offers several training modes, each targeting a distinct cognitive skill.

### 1. Echo Mode
A sequence is played. You reproduce it. The trainer compares your timing and structure against the reference.

### 2. Flash Mode
A character or callsign appears briefly. You must respond with the corresponding phonetic words.

### 3. Conversation Mode
Simulated exchanges — a fictional station transmits; you reply in Morse or phonetics. Great for building situational fluency.

### 4. Marathon Mode
Long-session endurance drills that track fatigue and suggest break intervals to protect against burnout.

### 5. Zen Mode
No scoring, no timers, no pressure. Just a continuous stream of signals and your quiet responses. Ideal for winding down.

### 6. Exam Mode
Timed assessments that mimic amateur radio licensing conditions, with configurable pass thresholds.
